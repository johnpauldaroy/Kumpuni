# Kumpuni mockup review

Reviewed the 1440 px desktop and 390 px phone layouts, scroll sheets and comparison sheets. The browser reported no mechanical problems. Desktop tests observed three ScrollTriggers and Lenis. Reduced motion stopped both the scroll sequences and smooth scrolling.

Hero reads as: a desktop laser printer beside a diagnostic action

Score against Stay22: 8.4/10
Score against MusicWave.ai: 8.6/10
Score against CSS Design Awards honorable mention: 8.2/10

Score for hierarchy: 9/10
Score for balance: 8.8/10
Score for originality: 8.2/10
Score for polish: 8.6/10
Score for depth: 8.3/10
Score for premium: 8.3/10

Stay22 puts brand color around navigation and action. Kumpuni concentrates it in a queue panel with enough neutral space for status. MusicWave.ai contrasts art with rows of product details. Kumpuni uses that contrast between a single printer photo and plain inventory.

## Weakest three

- The preselected Office demo allows people to start without seeing the one-time setup dialog.
- The mobile hero copy overlaps part of the photo and relies on the dark overlay.
- The console uses three example queues, so the visual density of a large print host remains untested.

## Independent judge

Score against the independent judge: 8.6/10

A reviewer who did not implement the mockup judged the desktop and phone captures, comparing the actual Stay22 and MusicWave.ai pages. They confirmed that the mobile queue rows preserve the per-queue action. They flagged that running a check on a phone should scroll to show progress. The implementation brings the results panel into view when needed.
