# Website brief

Request: A flat, modern, web-style mockup for the Kumpuni desktop app

## Business
"Kumpuni is a Windows printer diagnostics and repair app."

## Facts to use word for word
Kumpuni
Kumpuni is a Windows printer diagnostics and repair app.

## References
None given by the user. Reviewed references:
- GitHub Primer Action List (https://primer.style/product/components/action-list/): visible active navigation and concise action labels.
- IBM Carbon Progress Indicator (https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/core/components/progress-indicator/guidelines): ordered stages and distinct current and completed states.
- Stay22 and MusicWave.ai: captured software-site references for restrained color and section pacing.

## Style
Their words: "more modern UI flat and just like web pages"
A flat product interface with neutral surfaces, a single forest-green accent, and a clear hierarchy.

## Wow level
full (Full show: A huge picture or live 3D, rich motion)

## Goal
Their words: "one time setup then less buttons just one click there are diagnosis then solve the diagnosis"

## What they have
Their words: "Kumpuni logo and green color palette"

## What to make
Reuse the supplied logo and green palette. Pair them with a wordmark in the display face.
Show the product UI at full size. Use motion to illustrate setup, the diagnostic sequence, and the repair result.

## Pages
One browser page. Its states show the saved printer, optional setup, a simulated repair, and technician inventory and reports.

## Tone
Plain operational language with short next steps and clear results.

## Assumptions
This is a clickable desktop-app mockup. The app shell is the main visual.
Printer names, queue states and repair results are labeled demo data.
The prototype simulates repairs in the browser and uses no Windows APIs.
