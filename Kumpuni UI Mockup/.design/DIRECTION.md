Category: utility
Signature: photograph

## Concept

The overview anchors the saved printer and its connection around one Check and fix action. Setup is available on first use. The console puts queue, driver and port lists alongside status and repair reports.

## Rejected first idea

A marketing home page would lead with copy and delay the actual repair controls. The printer image sits inside the action area so the queue and result panels remain visible.

## References

- Stay22, https://www.stay22.com: concentrated color around the main action and distinct visual anchor.
- MusicWave.ai, https://www.musicwave.ai/: contrast between a bold first screen and structured product details below.

Primer's Action List and Carbon's Progress Indicator support practical navigation and ordered status states.

## Signature moment

A full-height printer photograph occupies the right of the forest-green queue panel. The saved printer's name and Check and fix take the left side. A deeper mobile overlay protects legibility.
